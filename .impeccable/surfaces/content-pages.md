# Work and Writing

Mode: Read. These dedicated pages let visitors understand Ragesh’s work and thinking before reaching out. Targets: work.html and writing.html.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give each reader a named place to go and a useful next step. Keep project links and Medium previews visible, with consistent navigation back to the wider site.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Source Serif 4/Source Sans 3, sky-to-paper colors, blue links, quiet rules, and the day/evening switch. Shared header/footer across all main and project pages; active-section indication and project breadcrumbs.

ALIGNMENT: Use the shared 980px header/footer shell and 660px reading measure. Writing uses the centered reading column, with article titles and excerpts aligned beside consistent thumbnail columns. Work keeps its 980px experience/project grids while introductions, heading groups, FAQs, and closing prose use the shared reading measure. All nine project pages align headings, prose, uncropped photographs, and video embeds in one 660px column. Reading prose is justified on desktop and left-aligned at 600px and below; cards and compact summaries stay naturally aligned. Gutters are 32px desktop and 20px mobile.

STORY: Work presents professional experience, nine project entry points, and collaboration options. Writing presents the Medium feed first, then additional technical writing and the ROSCon talk. Closing sections invite another relevant page or a LinkedIn conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A clear page title and purpose, current-page navigation, then meaningful content. Work has local links to experience, projects, and working together; Writing exposes the latest articles and filters directly.

FORM: Established-world content migration, seed: none (pinned): “everything else should be redirected from here”. Existing project URLs, story prose, photo, publication links, and Medium refresh behavior are retained. The source templates and shared renderer keep navigation synchronized.

FINISH: Verify navigation and content routes across every page, old-fragment forwarding, visible articles/projects, keyboard navigation, mobile wrapping, and LinkedIn contact. Do not claim a measured conversion uplift.
