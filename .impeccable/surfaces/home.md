Mode: Persuade. The owner’s latest instruction is a story-first reading path: My story → Work → How I work → Writing → Medium subscription or LinkedIn connection. The homepage contains only the hero, global navigation, and footer. Only LinkedIn and YouTube open in new tabs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Understand who Ragesh is and begin with his story. Each reading page recommends a next step; subscription and connection stay available everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Soft sky fading into off-white paper, restrained blue links, Source Serif 4 positioning headline, Source Sans 3 body copy, and a 980px editorial layout. Evening changes the paper to deep blue and links to pale blue. The discovery action and compact speaking portrait provide hierarchy.

STORY: Meet a robotics engineer and founder whose work makes robots dependable. Begin with My story, continue through Work and How I work to Writing, then subscribe on Medium or connect on LinkedIn. No invented outcomes, availability, or current-employer identifiers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared navigation with current-page indication; “I build dependable systems for robotics and physical AI”; concise first-person positioning; “Start with my story” as the primary action and a quieter Writing shortcut. Supporting copy explains the path toward Medium or LinkedIn. The ROSCon speaker photo and talk link sit beside the copy on desktop. On mobile, a compact horizontal photo-and-identity row follows the introduction and precedes the discovery links; retain the rectangular portrait and visible talk link. The hero ends with discovery. No long sections follow.

FORM: Code-led continuation of the established editorial identity. Dedicated static pages replace homepage scrolling: about.html, work.html, how-i-work.html, writing.html. Shared navigation is visible without a menu interaction. Medium previews and project links are directly visible on their pages. End-of-page links recommend the next page or lead to the final Medium/LinkedIn invitation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
