# Hero-only personal homepage

Mode: Experience. The owner wants like-minded peers to meet the person behind the work and finds the current site too impersonal and formal. Retain the story-first reading path: My story → Work → How I work → Writing → Medium subscription or LinkedIn connection. The homepage contains only the hero, global navigation, and footer. Only LinkedIn and YouTube open in new tabs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Meet Ragesh through a direct greeting, his real engineering journey, and his people-first outlook. The introduction should feel like the start of a conversation with a fellow builder. Each reading page recommends a next step; subscription and connection stay available everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Soft sky fading into off-white paper, restrained blue links, Source Serif 4 positioning headline, Source Sans 3 body copy, and a 980px editorial layout. Evening changes the paper to deep blue and links to pale blue. The discovery action and compact speaking portrait provide hierarchy.

STORY: Meet a robotics engineer and founder who cares about the people behind the systems. His existing principles supply the personal voice: people first, simple systems, honest conversations, and sharing knowledge. Begin with My story, continue through Work and How I work to Writing, then subscribe on Medium or connect on LinkedIn. No invented interests, outcomes, availability, or current-employer identifiers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared navigation with current-page indication; “Hi, I’m Ragesh.” in the established display face; the owner-retained tagline “I build dependable systems for robotics and physical AI.” prominently below it; concise factual background and a link into his people-first working philosophy. The tagline’s technical terms also belong in the accurate page description. “Start with my story” is the primary action with a quieter Writing shortcut. The ROSCon photograph and talk link sit beside the copy on desktop. On mobile, a compact horizontal photo-and-caption row follows the introduction and precedes the discovery links; navigation wraps deliberately, and the story action fits within a normal phone viewport. The hero ends with discovery. No long sections follow.

FORM: Code-led refinement of the established editorial identity and two-column hero. Retain the current type families, palette, 980px shell, 660px reading measure, and day/evening interaction. Dedicated static pages hold the deeper content. Shared navigation is visible without a menu interaction. Medium previews and project links are directly visible on their pages. End-of-page links recommend the next page or lead to the final Medium/LinkedIn invitation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
