How I can help your robotics team.

I help teams improve how they release software, deploy updates, and keep robots running. Start with a review of your current setup, then decide what to build and where you need support.

Deployment Readiness Assessment

If releases rely on manual steps, failed updates are hard to recover from, or you’re preparing to add more robots, I can review how software moves from a code change to your fleet. Together, we identify the risks and decide what to improve first.

  • A map of how code is built, tested, and deployed to your robots.
  • A prioritized list of gaps in deployment, failure recovery, and fleet monitoring.
  • An implementation plan showing what to change and in what order.
  • A walkthrough with your engineers to discuss the findings and next steps.

Hands-on implementation

Part-time Robotics DevOps leadership

Explore the technical scope
ROS / ROS 2 CI/CD & release engineering
Design and implement build, test, packaging, and release pipelines. Establish versioned artifacts and reproducible environments so a release can be identified, deployed, and recovered consistently.
Fleet deployment & OTA updates
Build deployment pipelines and over-the-air update mechanisms for on-premise, hybrid, and cloud-connected robots. Plan staged rollouts, compatibility checks, and recovery paths around real hardware and network constraints.
Observability & incident response
Introduce logs, metrics, and operational dashboards that make fleet health visible. Focus on uptime, mission success rates, rollout health, and a clear process for diagnosing and responding to failures.

Questions about working together.

When is it a good time to work together?

When your robots work in pilots but releases, customer-site deployments, or field incidents are becoming harder to manage. The assessment is a starting point for teams preparing to expand a fleet or improve an existing deployment workflow.

Do you implement changes or only advise?

Both are possible. An assessment produces findings and an implementation roadmap. A separately scoped implementation engagement can cover release pipelines, OTA workflows, observability, and team handover. Part-time leadership is an option for ongoing technical direction.

Can you work with our existing team and infrastructure?

Yes. The starting point is your current robot platform, release process, and team. My experience covers ROS and ROS 2, on-premise, hybrid, and cloud-connected environments. We agree the access and engineering involvement needed for the scope before starting.

How are scope, timing, and fees agreed?
