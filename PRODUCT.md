## Product Purpose
The primary visitor journey is Home → My story → Work → How I work → Writing → subscribe on Medium or connect on LinkedIn. The owner wants purposeful reading with a clear next step on every page. Home remains hero-only and starts with “Start with my story”; My story explains his journey with earlier robotics projects woven into the relevant chapters; Work presents professional experience and collaboration options; How I work helps hiring teams and collaborators assess fit; Writing hosts the visible Medium feed, notes, talks, and the final subscription/connection invitation. “Stay in touch” in the shared header leads to that invitation. Visitors can also use direct navigation or the footer’s Medium/LinkedIn links. On Medium, subscription means following the profile and enabling email notifications; do not promise an automatic subscription or a publishing cadence. Search and answer-engine discovery remains a supporting goal. Do not turn the homepage back into a long consulting sales page or make an assessment request its primary action.

## Users

Use conversational introductions and make the owner's existing people-first philosophy, simplicity, candor, and shared learning easier to discover. Prospective collaborators, robotics founders, CTOs, engineering leaders, VC scouts, and technical readers learning about Ragesh’s experience and thinking. Some visitors need help scaling deployments; others arrive through his writing or want to understand the person behind the work. Make exploration clear before asking for contact.

The Work page’s implementation and leadership offers target high-value engagements with robotics companies in the EU and US. Address founders and CTOs scaling from pilots to production fleets through technical ownership, customer commitments, operational risk, infrastructure costs, and delivery capability. Keep the voice personal and concrete; no pricing, guaranteed savings, regional client claims, or expanded certification claims have been supplied.
